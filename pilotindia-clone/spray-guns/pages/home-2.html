FAQs

1. Which pilot spray gun should I use to spray a car?

For automotive painting, go with a high‑precision HVLP or professional series like Pilot’s Evolution or Legacy range. These give fine atomization, smooth coverage, and consistent finishes ideal for base coats and top coats on cars. 

2. How do I clean a spray paint gun after spraying?

Clean your spray gun right after use to prevent build‑up. Empty any leftover paint, disassemble key parts like the nozzle, air cap, and needle, then rinse and brush them with the appropriate solvent. Dry thoroughly before putting everything back together. 

3. What paint/coatings can I use with the Pilot Spray Gun?

The Pilot Spray Gun works with a wide range of coatings, stains, primers, topcoats, and many automotive paints.

4. How long does each nozzle last?

On the Pilot Spray Gun, each nozzle typically lasts about 150–200 hours of refinishing work. That usually equals roughly 15–20 average jobs, though actual life depends on use and materials.
